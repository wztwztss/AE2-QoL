# Infinite Water & Lava Cell

An ME storage cell that **never runs out**: preloaded with `Long.MAX_VALUE` (~9.2 × 10^18 mB) of both water and lava.

## Use Cases

- Endless water/lava supply for your AE network: fluid interfaces, pneumatic machines, lava boilers — extract forever.

## Crafting

Water bucket + lava bucket (see recipe tab).

## Usage

1. Put it into an ME Drive.
2. Any device that pulls water or lava from the network can now extract without limit.
3. Storing items is safe — only those two fluids appear infinite.
